Sourdough timing (sidebar entry, not a message)

I want to plan drip irrigation for a 6 by 3 metre vegetable bed. Where should I start?

A drip system for an 18 square metre bed is a good weekend project. Start with water needs, then layout.

Water needs

Most vegetables need about 25 mm of water per week in mild weather.

Emitter spacing

  • Use pressure-compensating emitters every 30 cm.
  • Run one line per crop row.

Layout

total_flow_l_per_h = emitters * 2

What parts do I need from the tap to the bed?

Parts list

  1. A backflow preventer at the tap.
  2. A filter and a pressure regulator.
  3. 16 mm supply tubing and fittings.

Order the backflow preventer first; it protects drinking water.

How long should I run it each day?

With 40 emitters at 2 L/h, a 30 minute run delivers about 40 litres.

SeasonMinutes per day
Spring20
Summer35

Can I automate it with a timer?

Timer options

A battery tap timer is the simplest choice. Pair it with the pressure-compensating emitters so flow stays even.

What about winter?

Winterizing

Drain the lines, remove the filter screen and store the timer indoors before the first frost.
