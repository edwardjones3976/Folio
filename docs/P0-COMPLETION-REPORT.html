What is stored (changed)

Each bookmark anchor now also stores context.before and context.after: the role and 16-hex content digest of the neighbouring messages, not their text. A malformed context read back from storage is dropped, which makes that anchor resolve as ambiguous rather than exact.

Known gaps

  • Live claude.ai behaviour is UNKNOWN.
  • A message at the top of what is loaded is confirmed only by the next message; if that is missing or regenerated, the target is ambiguous until more of the chat loads.
  • Bookmarks saved by Folio 0.1.0 have no context and report ambiguous; they must be bookmarked again.
  • No conversation-global ordinal is stored: Folio cannot prove that the first loaded message is the conversation start, so it does not claim one.
  • When the loaded window could sit in two equally good places among known messages, Folio keeps only the loaded messages; messages seen earlier are then no longer listed or searchable.
  • Deleting a bookmark whose id also appears in a record that failed validation (a duplicate) leaves that record; on the next read it may become the valid bookmark with that id.
  • Erase all reaches tabs where Folio's content script runs; tabs opened before installation have nothing to erase.
  • Only loaded messages are indexed; other branch versions, images, files and artifacts are not. Chapters are partial. No archive or export of conversations (P1).
  • The real side-panel container, toolbar click, screen readers, theme visuals and JSON download to disk are not covered by automation.
  • No TypeScript type-check was run (no compiler available).
